About the author

From pain to purpose.

Muhammad Sow is the author of A Thousand Miles of Tears: My Journey From Ashes to Light, a deeply personal account of hardship, resilience, transformation, and hope.

His writing explores the roads we never expected to travel, the wounds we carry, and the strength that can emerge when we choose to keep walking.
